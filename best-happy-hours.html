AFTER-CLASS FAVORITE

1. The Downtown Social

Happy Hour: Monday–Friday, 3–6 PM

Sample Deals: $4 mocktails, $5 appetizers, and $3 soft drinks.

Why It Stands Out: A relaxed atmosphere and affordable options make this fictional location ideal for meeting friends after class.

LATER HAPPY HOUR

2. The Corner Lounge

Sample Deals: $5 cocktails, $4 draft beers, and $6 shareable appetizers.

Why It Stands Out: This fictional spot offers a mix of food and drink deals for students planning an evening downtown.

EARLY AFTERNOON DEALS

The best choice depends on what you're looking for. The Downtown Social has inexpensive appetizers, The Corner Lounge offers later happy hour times, and The Hawkeye Hangout has early afternoon specials.

Whether you're comparing prices, looking for a place to meet friends, or planning an afternoon out, these sample locations show how different happy hour options could fit different budgets and schedules.

Looking for the best happy hour in Iowa City? This fictional guide compares sample food and drink specials designed for college students and young adults on a budget. All locations, prices, and deals below are invented for this class project.

1. The Downtown Social

Happy Hour: Monday–Friday, 3–6 PM

Sample Deals: $4 mocktails, $5 appetizers, and $3 soft drinks.

Why It Stands Out: A relaxed atmosphere and affordable options make this fictional location ideal for meeting friends after class.

2. The Corner Lounge

Happy Hour: Tuesday–Friday, 4–7 PM

Sample Deals: $5 cocktails, $4 draft beers, and $6 shareable appetizers.

Why It Stands Out: This fictional spot offers a mix of food and drink deals for students planning an evening downtown.

3. The Hawkeye Hangout

Happy Hour: Wednesday–Saturday, 2–5 PM

Sample Deals: $3 lemonades, $5 nachos, and $4 drink specials.

Why It Stands Out: This fictional location offers inexpensive food and a casual setting for a group outing.

# A/B Test Plan – Iowa City Happy Hour Guide

## Page and Element
I will test the main headline on the homepage (index.html). Version A will use the current homepage headline, "Iowa City Happy Hour Guide." Version B will use "Find Affordable Happy Hour Deals in Iowa City." The headline will be the only element changed between versions.

## Hypothesis
I predict that Version B will receive higher ratings for likelihood of using the website because it directly emphasizes affordable deals. College students looking for happy hour options may find this wording more useful and relevant.

## Model, Persona, and Prompt
I will use the same ChatGPT model and settings for all trials. Each AI visitor will be in a fresh conversation with no memory of previous trials.

Persona: A University of Iowa student looking for affordable happy hour deals in Iowa City.

Exact prompt: "You are a University of Iowa student looking for affordable happy hour deals in Iowa City. Read the following website homepage. On a scale of 1 to 7, how likely would you be to use this website to find a happy hour deal? Respond with one number and one sentence explaining your rating."

I will provide the full homepage text with either Version A or Version B's headline. I will randomly assign each trial to Version A or Version B before running it and record the assignment.

## Primary Outcome
The primary outcome will be the likelihood-of-use rating from 1 to 7. Higher scores indicate a greater likelihood of using the website.

## Sample Size and Timeline
I will conduct 100 total trials, with 50 assigned to Version A and 50 to Version B. I will finish collecting data by November 17, 2026. I will collect all 100 responses before evaluating the results.

## Comparison and Decision
I will calculate the average rating for each version and compare the difference using a statistical test. My hypothesis will be supported if Version B has a higher average rating than Version A and the difference is statistically significant at the 0.05 level. I will report all responses, including results that do not support my hypothesis.

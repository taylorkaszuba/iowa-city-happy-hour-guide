Good Drinks, Better Prices ✨

Looking for cheap drinks in Iowa City? This educational guide features fictional happy hour drink specials designed to show how college students and young adults could compare affordable options. All businesses and prices below are invented.

🥤
NONALCOHOLIC FAVORITES

The Downtown Social

Happy Hour: Monday–Friday, 3–6 PM

Sample Drink Specials: $3 soft drinks and $4 mocktails.

Best For: Students looking for affordable nonalcoholic drinks after class.

🍸
EVENING DRINK SPECIALS

The Corner Lounge

Happy Hour: Tuesday–Friday, 4–7 PM

Sample Drink Specials: $4 draft beers and $5 cocktails.

Best For: Adults 21 and older looking for sample budget-friendly bar specials.

🍋
AFTERNOON FAVORITES

The Hawkeye Hangout

Happy Hour: Wednesday–Saturday, 2–5 PM

Sample Drink Specials: $3 lemonades and $4 drink specials.

Best For: An inexpensive afternoon outing with friends.

Compare Drink Prices 💗

For nonalcoholic options, The Downtown Social and The Hawkeye Hangout have sample drinks starting at $3. The Corner Lounge features fictional alcoholic drink specials starting at $4.
